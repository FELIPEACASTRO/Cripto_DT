### 7.3 Indian Crypto Regulation (2025-2026)
- **Legal Status:** Crypto legally defined as Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) under Income Tax Act, 1961
- **Tax Rate:** Flat 30% on all crypto gains (no deductions except cost of acquisition)
- **TDS:** 1% Tax Deducted at Source on all VDA transfers (since July 1, 2022)
- **ITR Reporting:** From FY 2025-26, mandatory Schedule VDA in Income Tax Returns
- **Proposed Legislation:** COINS Act 2025 suggests regulation (not prohibition)
- **Expected Path:** Similar to Japan/Singapore -- licensing exchanges, taxing gains
- **Market Size:** India became world's largest crypto market in 2025 (119 million users)
- **Full Regulations:** Expected in 2026
- **Exchange Security:**
  - CoinDCX breach July 2025: customer assets safe, losses absorbed by exchange
  - WazirX: KYC, cold storage, insurance
- **Cannot be used as currency** -- only as investment asset

### 7.4 Indian Market Data APIs (Detailed)
- **NSE/BSE Official:** Limited direct API access; available through broker APIs
- **SEBI Mandate:** Static IP required for API-based trading from April 1, 2026
- **Free Options:**

  - Groww API -- live + historical
  - IndianAPI.in -- REST API for Indian stock market
- **150M+ equity investors** in India interacting with market apps

- **Global Adoption:** India leads 2026 Global Crypto Adoption Index

1. **Sentiment Pipeline:** IndicBERTv2/v3 -> fine-tune on IndicSentiment + financial domain data -> extract sentiment from Hindi/English crypto discussions
2. **Translation Layer:** IndicTrans2 (200M distilled for speed, 1B for quality) -> translate multi-language financial news to English for unified processing
3. **NER Pipeline:** IndicNER -> extract crypto entities, exchange names, regulatory entities from Indian news
